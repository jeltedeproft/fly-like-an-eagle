# Art Acceptance Contract

Before an asset can ship, reviewers verify all of the following:

1. It has a complete `AssetLedgerEntry` and explicit reuse permission.
2. It uses only approved master-palette colors or documented derived shades.
3. Outline weight, light direction, texture, scale, and silhouette match the art style profile.
4. At gameplay size it remains readable against every intended background.
5. In the style-test composition it appears authored with the vehicle, terrain, effect, and UI samples.
6. Animation and effects do not reduce access to critical gameplay information.

Failure at any step leaves the asset prototype-only, reference-only, or rejected. Recoloring alone is not
evidence of stylistic compatibility.
