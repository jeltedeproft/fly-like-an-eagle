# Scrap-yard Sunset Art Guide

All visuals use the 16-color `palette.hex`. The world is a hand-built, slightly grimy illustrated
scrap yard at sunset: chunky silhouettes, dark two-to-four-pixel outlines, flat two-step shading,
warm machinery against cool skies, and imperfect hand-painted geometry.

- Light comes from the upper left. Highlights are cream/sun; shadows shift toward coal, never black.
- Vehicles exaggerate fragile joints, mismatched wheels, tape, rivets, and wobble.
- UI resembles cream paper signs bolted onto dark metal. Gold is reserved for rewards and purchases.
- Effects use solid graphic shapes. No soft photographic textures, gradients, or unrelated pixel art.
- Animation comes from rotation, squash, rattling components, dust, sparks, and short camera impulses.
- Gameplay silhouettes and landing surfaces always outrank decoration.
